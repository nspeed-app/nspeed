doc: https://api.bbox.fr/doc/apirouter/#api-WAN

steps to get wan link specs:

1. https://mabbox.bytel.fr/api/v1/wan/ip
type = wan.link.type

then

2. https://mabbox.bytel.fr/api/v1/wan/<type>/stats
type=
 ffth |
 xdsl |

response: [{"wan":{"ftth":{"mode":"1000baseT-FD","state":"Up"}}}]

also
maxBandwidth from https://mabbox.bytel.fr/api/v1/wan/ip/stats for tx & rx

client to gateway:
https://mabbox.bytel.fr/api/v1/hosts/me

-> curl -s https://mabbox.bytel.fr/api/v1/hosts/me | jq ".[0].host.wireless"
   .host.link
   then
   .host.link.wireless |
   .host.link.ethernet

authentification:
curl -v -X POST -d password=******** https://mabbox.bytel.fr/api/v1/login
if OK  save cookie BBOX_ID

then use 
curl -s --cookie "BBOX_ID=...." https://mabbox.bytel.fr/api/v1/....
